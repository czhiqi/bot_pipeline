# Hypothesis: 4H EMA50/150 trend flips on BTC

## Idea
BTC trends persist for weeks. A flip that needs price, the fast EMA and a rising slow EMA to agree filters out most chop on the 4H chart.

## Expected effect
Few trades (roughly 6–8 a year), long only, with winners much larger than losers. Profit should come from a handful of long trends.

## What would prove it wrong
- Out-of-sample net ≤ 0, or out-of-sample profit factor below 1.05.
- Most profit coming from one or two trades in-sample and none out-of-sample.
- Drawdown larger than the out-of-sample profit (already a watch item on the Binance BTC flip bot).

## Risk
3x isolated, 5% stop (about −15% ROE), one entry, 0.001 BTC.
